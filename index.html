Vacation Scheduler

Plan your vacations and excursions in one place, get alerts on important dates, and share your itinerary from your Android phone.

Download for Android (APK)

Version 1.0 · Requires Android 8.0 or later

What the App Does

  • Create a private account. Passwords are stored as salted hashes, never as plain text.
  • Add, edit, and delete vacations, each with a title, vacation type, hotel, and dates.
  • Add, edit, and delete excursions for each vacation.
  • Search your vacations by title or hotel.
  • View a Vacation Summary Report with a date-time stamp.
  • Set alerts for vacation start and end dates and for excursion dates.
  • Share a vacation's details by email, text message, or other apps.

How to Install

  1. On your Android phone, open this page and tap Download for Android (APK).
  2. When the download finishes, open the file app-release.apk from the notification or from your Downloads folder.
  3. If Android asks for permission to install apps from this source, tap Settings, turn on Allow from this source, and go back.
  4. Tap Install, then tap Open.

This app is shared directly instead of through the Google Play Store, so Android asks for this permission once. You can turn the permission off again after the app is installed.

Getting Started

  1. Open Vacation Scheduler and tap New here? Create an account.
  2. Choose a username and a password with at least 8 characters, including a letter and a number.
  3. Tap View My Vacations, then Add Vacation to plan your first trip.
  4. Tap View Vacation Report on the home screen to see a summary of all your trips.
